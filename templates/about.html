FROM VIDEO TO STANDARD WORK

Make practical knowledge easier to follow, share, and maintain.

Training Video Workspace helps teams turn unstructured training recordings into timestamped transcripts, clear step-by-step instructions, and reviewable Standard Operating Procedures.

01

Capture the process

Upload a training recording and use local faster-whisper transcription to produce text segments with timestamps.

02

Structure the knowledge

Review transcript sections, edit instructions, and use a configurable AI provider to draft a procedure from source material.

03

Publish documentation

Review generated steps, correct uncertainties, and export a professional PDF with headings, timestamps, and page numbers.

Why it was developed

This workspace reduces the effort required to convert tacit, video-based know-how into consistent working documentation. It keeps instructions connected to the relevant moment in the source recording so reviewers can verify details instead of relying on memory.

How AI is used

Speech recognition creates a timestamped transcript. When configured, an external AI service organizes that transcript into an SOP draft, including process overview, purpose, prerequisites, warnings, ordered steps, expected outcomes, and summary. AI output is a draft—not an approved procedure—and uncertain or unsupported information should be reviewed by a person.

Typical workflow

  1. Upload a training video.
  2. Transcribe the audio and review timestamped segments.
  3. Edit transcript segments into actionable instructions.
  4. Generate an AI SOP draft when an AI API is configured.
  5. Review and edit the content, then download the PDF.
